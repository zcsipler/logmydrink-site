An estimate, not a measurement

LogMyDrink estimates blood alcohol from the drinks you log and the body details you enter, using a general model. Real values differ from person to person and from day to day. The app is not a breathalyser and not a medical device: it does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. Never use it to decide whether you can drive, operate machinery or do anything else where alcohol matters. For medical questions, ask a health professional.
